Big Data for Sydney

An analysis of SA2 regions using open source data from the Australian Bureau of Statistics.

Overview

During semester 1 of 2024, two colleagues (Justin Chor and Sarah Cantet) and myself undertook an assignment for DATA2001, Data Science, Big Data and Data Variety. The project involved analysing SA2 statistical regions across Greater Sydney using open data from the Australian Bureau of Statistics (ABS). The end goal was to identify the most bustling regions based on various metrics. I know, hilarious thing to measure. Kudos to Jarrod the TA of the course, of whom I had the privilege of working with on the teaching team as a tutor a year later. The work combines cleaning and aggregating spreadsheets or APIs with maps and charts to compare suburbs and regions on metrics such as population, median income, and number of businesses. The final deliverable was a report and presentation, which we submitted to the course.
