Better Planner

A reminders and calendar application from my high school software project, built with Swift and SwiftUI.

Calendar or reminder list

Overview

This was my HSC Software Design and Development major project. Better Planner helps everyone keep reminders and events in one place: create reminders, see what’s due, and navigate a calendar. Funnily enough, the original idea was for it to be a fully fledged planner app. Ultimately, due to time constraints and feature creep, I had to tone it down to what it ended up being – which is essentially a reminders app.
